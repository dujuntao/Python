# Anonymous Benchmark Card: Score Rescuability v1

## What is evaluated

Score Rescuability evaluates whether confidence/uncertainty scores preserve low-risk accepted target subsets under tabular distribution shift after target coverage is fixed.

## Metrics

For each row and score, the contract reports target coverage, source-validation selective risk, target selective risk, target full risk, source-target risk gap, bootstrap intervals when masks are available, seed stability, and artifact sensitivity labels.

## Dataset families

The v1 artifact contains 31 evidence rows across six public tabular, temporal, and domain-shift dataset families: Gas Sensor Drift, Electricity, Covertype, Online Shoppers, Folktables ACS, and Bank Marketing.

## Main claim boundary

Gas Sensor Drift is the current stable severe anchor. Electricity is partially score-rescuable. Covertype is retained as boundary evidence because severe-looking means fail the stability promotion gate. Online Shoppers, Folktables, and Bank Marketing define the negative/control and artifact-sensitive side of the benchmark.

## Experiment hardening status

The package includes `results/experiment_hardening/`, generated by `scripts/make_icdm_hardening_completion.py`. These tables document split isolation, target-label use, score/label negative controls, paired uncertainty, conformal sanity checks, accepted-mask provenance, Gas domain-artifact checks, external score-file demos, and reproducibility/compliance status.

The claim remains intentionally bounded: Covertype is non-Gas boundary evidence, not a replacement stable severe anchor. The current broad public-tabular claim should be read as a benchmark landscape, not as prevalence evidence that all tabular shifts are score-resistant.

## Reuse interface

New methods can export one score per source-validation example and one score per target example. The evaluator fixes target coverage and computes the same target-risk contract.
